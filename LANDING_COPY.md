## FAQ

### Is this a procurement tool?

No. It is a fast audit that shows whether your current AI stack is roughly right-sized and where a deeper procurement conversation makes sense.

### Do I need to enter an email first?

No. The audit result appears first. Email capture is optional and only used to save the report or route high-savings cases.

### How accurate are the savings?

The engine uses current public pricing and conservative rules. It does not assume every team should switch tools, and it shows when your stack is already reasonable.

### Why does Credex appear in some audits?

Credex helps companies access discounted AI infrastructure credits. The tool surfaces Credex more prominently when the audit finds enough savings to justify a consultation.

### Is my public report private?

The public share URL strips email, company name, and role. It only shows tool categories, spend numbers, recommendations, and savings.
