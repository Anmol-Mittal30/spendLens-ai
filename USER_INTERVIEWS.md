## Fast Interview Script

1. What AI tools does your team pay for every month?
2. Who notices when the bill changes?
3. Have you ever downgraded, switched, or cancelled an AI tool because of price?
4. What would make you trust a recommendation to change plans?
5. Would you share an anonymized audit if it showed useful savings?
